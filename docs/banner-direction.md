Background deep navy with subtle structured grid of fine lines, almost invisible; elegant soft studio highlights around panels, no gaudy glow. Keep all typography exact, well within safe margins and readable when scaled to 850px wide. Only those three strings; no extra words, logos, branding, watermarks, stock photos, robots, brains, unnecessary arrows, busy decoration, or exaggerated 3D.
